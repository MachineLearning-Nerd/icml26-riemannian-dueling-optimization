# Limitations and deviations

- Monte Carlo checks three finite dimensions; the analytic expectation is the
  independent checker for arbitrary positive integer dimension.
- The finite-`nu` route falsifies the registry's unqualified estimator wording,
  not Lemma 3.2's ideal-vector identity.
- A finite counterexample does not contradict Lemma 3.1's stated
  high-probability small-perturbation guarantee.
- Runtime is for the cumulative legacy suite, not Claim 4 in isolation.
