than re-evaluating Table 1. It verifies the nonconvex optimization and
smoothing terms each equal epsilon/2, the convex contradiction consumes D at
the claimed horizon, and the exact RDFW batch sum is quadratic in T.

literal printed algorithm. The proof-consistent exponent is the control. Both
use identical objective, projection, phase count, phase length, oracle, and
